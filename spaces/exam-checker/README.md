# 📝 Instructor Essentials — OMR Exam Grading & Item Analysis

An open-source, computer-vision grading pipeline that evaluates scanned multiple-choice exam bubble sheets, matches student IDs against class rosters, and generates psychometric item analysis reports.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start in this Space

1. **Test with 1-Click Built-in Sample:**
   - Keep **"Use Built-in BIO 101 Midterm Sample if empty"** checked.
   - Click **"⚡ Pre-fill Inputs with Sample Files"** or directly click **"🔍 Grade Scanned Exams with Checker"**.
   - Within seconds, Checker will rectify all 10 sample scans, score them against the answer key, and render:
     - Cohort statistics (Mean score, Pass Rate, Cronbach's Alpha test reliability, Median, Std Dev).
     - Roster table with student scores and Pass/Fail statuses.
     - Visual gallery of annotated scans showing correct/incorrect bubbles highlighted in green/red.
     - Full interactive HTML Item Analysis Report embedding.
     - Downloadable ZIP archive containing all annotated scan images.

2. **Grade Your Own Custom Exams:**
   - **Upload Scans:** Upload your scanned JPEG or PNG exam images (single sheet or bulk batch).
   - **Upload Layout JSON:** The layout geometry generated by Sheet Builder (`layout.json`).
   - **Upload Answer Key JSON:** Your question answer key mapping item numbers to correct answers and point values.
   - **Upload Class Roster CSV (Optional):** A CSV containing `Student ID` and `Full Name` columns for automatic student attribution.
   - Click **"🔍 Grade Scanned Exams with Checker"**.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Under the Hood

- **Fiducial Rectification:** Employs 4 corner ArUco markers (`cv2.aruco.DICT_4X4_50`) to compute a 4-point perspective transform, correcting rotation, skew, perspective distortion, and scale changes without requiring mechanical scanner calibration.
- **Adaptive Bubble Fill Detection:** Uses morphological filtering and dynamic Otsu thresholding to distinguish filled bubbles from empty ones, remaining robust against light pencil marks, dark ink pens, and imperfect erasures.
- **Psychometrics Suite:**
  - **Item Difficulty ($P$-value):** Proportion of students who answered correctly.
  - **Item Discrimination ($r_{\text{pbis}}$):** Point-biserial correlation between item performance and total exam score.
  - **Reliability Index:** Cohort internal consistency via Cronbach's $\alpha$.
  - **Distractor Analysis:** Frequency distribution across all distractors (A, B, C, D, E).

## 🔒 Privacy & Student Records

When grading real student examinations or rosters subject to educational privacy regulations (e.g. FERPA):
- Host this Space as **Private** in your Hugging Face account settings to ensure student names, IDs, and scans are not publicly accessible.
- Alternatively, run Checker locally on your machine (`./checker` or `./checker-ui`) or in your browser (`docs/checker.html`) where 100% of processing remains on your local hardware.
